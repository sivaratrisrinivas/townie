# Assets

Every visual asset in Townie, where it came from, its license, and why it is there. All are recoloured to the bone, graphite and ultramarine palette and are marked `aria-hidden`.

| Screen | Asset | Source | License | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Joke | Comic on stage, calm and mid-sentence faces (`public/art/comic-*.svg`) | Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley, https://www.openpeeps.com, rendered from the npm package @opeepsfun/open-peeps (https://github.com/opeepsfun/open-peeps) | Open Peeps artwork: CC0 1.0. npm package: MIT | The comic you are writing for starts talking as you type. |
| 1 Joke | Microphone icon (inline SVG) | Lucide `mic`, https://lucide.dev/icons/mic | ISC | Leans toward the comic while you type. |
| 2 City | US and India outlines (`public/art/map-*.svg`) | Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas, https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas | Natural Earth: public domain. world-atlas: ISC | Shows the two countries covered and which one you are in. |
| 2 City | Map pin (inline SVG) | Lucide `map-pin`, https://lucide.dev/icons/map-pin | ISC | Drops onto tonight's city when the name matches. |
| Loading | Waveform (inline SVG) | Lucide `audio-lines`, https://lucide.dev/icons/audio-lines | ISC | A mic check that breathes while Qloo answers. |
| 3 Reveal | Laughing crowd (`public/art/laugh-*.svg`) | Open Peeps (as above) | CC0 1.0 / MIT | The room laughs as the local word seats. |
| 3 Reveal | Paper burst | canvas-confetti, https://github.com/catdad/canvas-confetti | ISC | One short burst for the aha, loaded only on the reveal. |
| 4 Why | Chart icon (inline SVG) | Lucide `chart-no-axes-column-increasing`, https://lucide.dev | ISC | Marks the set list as Qloo evidence. |
| 4 Why | Lift bars | Drawn in code from Qloo lift data | MIT (this repo) | Shows how much more local each pick is. |
